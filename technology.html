Our Technology

SafexGuard's technology is based on biomarker sensing. When food spoils or ripens, it releases specific chemical markers. Our sensing inks detect these markers and change colour, providing a clear, real-time signal of food quality.

Key Biomarkers We Detect

Biogenic Amines

Associated with protein breakdown in meat, seafood and some prepared foods.

Ethylene

A ripening hormone released by many fruits and vegetables.

Mercaptans

Sulfur-containing compounds associated with spoilage in certain products.

How It Works

  1. Food begins to spoil or ripen and releases biomarker gases or liquids.
  2. A SafexGuard sensing ink or pad, integrated into packaging or placed alongside the product, reacts to the biomarkers.
  3. The indicator changes colour, for example from colourless to blue or green to orange.
  4. Staff or consumers can assess freshness quickly without complex equipment.

Advantages

  • Real-time monitoring: reflects actual product condition, not only time since production.
  • Biomarker-based indication: responds to chemical changes associated with ripening or spoilage.
  • No power required: suitable for cold rooms, chillers and freezers.
  • Easy visual interpretation: a clear colour change can support day-to-day quality decisions.

Integration Options

  • Printed labels on packaging
  • Sensing pads placed under fresh meat or seafood
  • Combined absorbent and sensing pads for fresh-meat trays
  • Custom formats for client-specific packaging and production lines
