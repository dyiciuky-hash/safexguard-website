Applications Across the Food Supply Chain

SafexGuard's freshness-monitoring solutions support safer, smarter operations from farm to table.

Cold Chain & Logistics

Monitor perishable goods such as seafood, meat, and dairy during transport and storage, providing early alerts for temperature abuse or spoilage risk.

Retail & Supermarkets

Give retailers and shoppers a simple visual indicator of freshness, helping reduce food waste and improve consumer confidence.

Food Manufacturing

Integrate freshness sensing into packaging lines to strengthen quality control and support shelf-life claims.

Restaurants & Food Service

Help kitchens track ingredient freshness, reducing spoilage-related losses and supporting food safety compliance.

Import & Export

Support quality assurance for cross-border shipments of perishable products, offering objective, real-time freshness data.

Research & Development

Enable food scientists and packaging developers to study spoilage patterns and validate new smart-packaging designs.
