Products

SafexGuard offers smart freshness-monitoring products for different food categories and applications.

Smart Freshness Labels

For: Fruit, vegetables, chilled and frozen products.

Features

  • Detects ethylene and other ripening or spoilage markers
  • Colour change indicates ripeness or spoilage level
  • Can include a colour scale for internal quality-control staff

Benefits

  • Supports stock rotation and promotion decisions
  • Reduces premature disposal of still-usable products
  • Improves confidence in freshness management

Fresh Meat Sensing Pads

For: Pork, beef, chicken, seafood and other fresh meat products.

Features

  • Combines traditional absorbent functionality with chemical sensing
  • Designed to detect biogenic amines associated with meat spoilage
  • Uses high-contrast colour change for rapid visual checking

Benefits

  • Provides real-time freshness indication for fresh meat
  • Supports quality control in supermarkets, butcher shops and restaurants
  • Helps reduce waste and prevent the sale or serving of spoiled products

Custom Sensing Inks & Solutions

For: Food manufacturers, packaging companies and R&D partners.

Features

  • Tailored sensing inks for selected biomarkers and food types
  • Integration into existing labels and packaging formats
  • Collaborative development for new applications

Benefits

  • Enables tailored freshness-monitoring solutions
  • Supports smart-packaging innovation and food-safety initiatives

Pricing & Availability

SafexGuard products are available for pilot projects and commercial deployment. Standard and customised formats can be discussed based on product type, required sensitivity, packaging format and order volume.
