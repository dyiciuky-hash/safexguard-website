REAL-TIME FOOD FRESHNESS MONITORING

Real-time Food Freshness Monitoring for a Safer, Less Wasteful Future

SafexGuard develops smart labels and sensing pads that detect food spoilage in real time, helping businesses reduce waste, protect reputations and enhance food safety from farm to table.

Explore Our Technology Contact Us

Key Problems We Solve

  • Food waste remains a major challenge, partly because many operations lack real-time freshness monitoring.
  • Products may be discarded early as a precaution based only on printed expiry dates.
  • Some products can spoil before their expected expiry date, creating food-safety and reputational risks.
  • Retailers and consumers may find it difficult to assess ripeness and freshness accurately by appearance alone.

Our Solution at a Glance

SafexGuard uses biomarker-sensing technology to monitor food quality in real time.

Detect

Detects spoilage markers such as biogenic amines, ethylene and mercaptans.

Indicate

Changes colour when food begins to spoil or ripen.

Integrate

Works in chilled and frozen environments without a power supply and can be integrated into packaging workflows.
