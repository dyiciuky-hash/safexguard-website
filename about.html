About SafexGuard

SafexGuard is a food-tech company focused on real-time freshness monitoring and food-safety solutions. We develop smart labels, sensing inks and absorbent pads that detect spoilage markers and provide clear visual signals when food quality declines.

Our Mission

To reduce food waste and enhance food safety by making real-time freshness monitoring accessible, affordable and easy to adopt across the food supply chain.

Our Story

SafexGuard was founded by a team with chemistry and research backgrounds from City University of Hong Kong. We recognised that expiry-date systems can be imprecise: some products are discarded while still usable, while others may deteriorate earlier than expected.

We developed chemosensing inks that react to biomarker gases and liquids released during spoilage and ripening. These inks form the core of our smart labels and sensing pads, providing an objective, real-time indication of food quality.

Our Team

  • Founders and R&D staff with expertise in chemistry, materials science and food technology
  • Academic and industry advisors in food safety, packaging and supply-chain operations
  • A commercial team focused on pilots, partnerships and expansion in Hong Kong, the Greater Bay Area and Southeast Asia

Our Vision

A world where food waste is significantly reduced and food safety is enhanced through simple, intelligent monitoring tools integrated into everyday packaging and operations.
